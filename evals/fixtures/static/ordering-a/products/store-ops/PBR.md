# Product brief: store-ops

**Outcome.** Store managers see, in the morning, the three things that will cost them
money today: a stockout, a shift gap, a fridge that drifted overnight.

**For whom.** 140 store managers at Alveare Retail.

**Capabilities.** A morning digest. Alert acknowledgement. Nothing else yet.

**Out of scope.** Shift scheduling itself.
