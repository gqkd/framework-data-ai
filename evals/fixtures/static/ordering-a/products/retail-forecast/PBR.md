# Product brief: retail-forecast

**Outcome.** The category buyers of a grocery chain stop rebuilding their weekly order
quantities in a spreadsheet, because the system proposes them.

**For whom.** Eleven category buyers at Alveare Retail, head office in Bologna.

**Capabilities.** Weekly demand forecast per SKU and store. Proposed order quantity.
An override that is recorded and fed back.

**Out of scope.** Fresh produce. Promotions planning.
